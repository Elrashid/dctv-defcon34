aftermarket ble car alarms

36 min · DEF CON 34 session · critical

Three of six aftermarket BLE car remote/alarm systems (KARR/QT, Carlink BTLR, Rockledge) have broken app-layer auth; two are trivial to exploit from any phone in BLE range, affecting ~2.2 M vehicles that bypass OEM security.
