From Disclosure to Defense: Rebuilding Vulnerability Management in the AI Era

54 min · John Banghart, Elizabeth Eigner, Sam Curry, Lindsey Szczerbicki · DEF CON 34 session · high

Frontier AI makes vuln discovery abundant (66–100k CVEs/year; Microsoft’s largest Patch Tuesday hit 622) while disclosure, triage, and patching still assume scarcity—panel says prioritize ruthlessly, scale defenders (especially OSS), and avoid policies that break coordinated disclosure.
